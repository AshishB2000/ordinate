# Office handbook

## Parking

Visitors park in the lot behind the main entrance. Employees need a permit from the front desk, which is free and issued on the first day. Bicycles are kept in the garage room on the ground floor, next to the showers.

## Kitchen and coffee

The kitchen on every floor has a dishwasher; please run it before you leave on Friday. Coffee beans are ordered every Monday and the machine is descaled once a month by the facilities team.

## Booking meeting rooms

Rooms are booked in the calendar. A booking that is not used in the first ten minutes is released. The large room on the third floor needs a day's notice because it doubles as the town hall space.

## Holidays and time off

Time off is requested in the people system. Public holidays follow the office country. A request longer than two weeks needs the manager's approval a month ahead.

## Expenses

Receipts are photographed and uploaded the same week. Travel is booked through the company agency. Meals on a work trip are reimbursed up to a daily limit that the people team publishes.
