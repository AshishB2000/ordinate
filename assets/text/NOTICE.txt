Text analytics data bundled with Ordinate (read by src/analysis/text/, main process only).

vader-lexicon.json
  The VADER sentiment lexicon (vaderSentiment 3.3.2) by C.J. Hutto, converted from
  vaderSentiment/vader_lexicon.txt in https://github.com/cjhutto/vaderSentiment
  (master, fetched 2026-09-27). Only the first two columns are kept: the token and
  its mean valence. MIT licence: VADER-LICENSE.txt.

  Hutto, C.J. & Gilbert, E.E. (2014). VADER: A Parsimonious Rule-based Model for
  Sentiment Analysis of Social Media Text. ICWSM-14.

stopwords-en.json, stopwords-es.json, stopwords-fr.json, stopwords-de.json
  The Snowball project's stop word lists (https://snowballstem.org/algorithms/
  <language>/stop.txt), comments stripped, NFC-normalised and lower-cased, in the
  list's own order. BSD 3-Clause licence: SNOWBALL-LICENSE.txt.
