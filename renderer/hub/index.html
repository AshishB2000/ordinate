No scorecards yet

            
A scorecard lists the numbers that matter, each with a target and an owner. Every period the app scores them — on track, at risk or off track — shows the change on the period before and a twelve-period trend, and rolls each group up. Step back through history with the period picker; nothing is stored but the targets.

            

              
            

          

        


        
        

          

            
Drivers move a metric’s inputs, never the stored data. Open one to tune it, or compare up to four side by side.

            

              
              
            

          

          

          

            

              

              

              

            

            
No scenarios yet

            
A scenario asks “what if?” of your metrics. Add drivers — a price up five percent, one region’s volume down three, a discount set to zero — and every metric is recomputed beside its baseline, with a chart of which driver moves it most. The stored data never changes.

No stories yet

            
A story is a document you read top to bottom — headings and prose around live charts and metrics, which recompute every time it opens. Type / in any empty line to add a chart, a metric, an image or a callout; present it a section at a time, or export it as a PDF.

A report turns a dashboard into a file you can send — PDF, PowerPoint or Word — with the app's own sentence under every chart. Open a dashboard and choose “Create report…” from its More actions menu; you can schedule it to regenerate into a folder on its own.
